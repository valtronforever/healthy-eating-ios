# Copy rules

Healthy Eating speaks like a calm, precise friend who respects your privacy. Short sentences, plain words, facts before feelings. US English, sentence case everywhere (titles, buttons, tabs).

## Voice

- Say what is true and what to do next. "Can't reach food.example.com. Your data is safe on your server." not "Oops! Something went wrong."
- Second person, present tense: "Your data stays on your server."
- No exclamation marks, no emoji, no jokes in system messages.
- Name the assistant and the source plainly: "via Claude", "from a photo".
- One idea per sentence. Buttons are verbs: "Get started", "Copy", "Retry", "Mark day as complete", "Turn on", "Delete meal".

## Neutral numbers

| Write | Never write |
| --- | --- |
| 1,200 kcal so far · Target 1,900–2,200 kcal | 700 kcal left · You're under! |
| Fat 86 g · Target 55–80 g | Over your fat limit |
| 70 kcal lower than the previous 30 days | Great job, you cut 70 kcal |
| −0.3 kg per week | You lost weight! / Weight up ⚠ |
| Logged 6 of the last 7 days | 6-day streak |
| 2 of 17 meals after 21:00 | Late-night snacking |
| Estimated, medium confidence | Inaccurate / Bad estimate |

- Over and under get the same words: "lower", "higher", "within the range", or just the numbers.
- Never call a food or a day good, bad, clean, junk, cheat or healthy.
- Weight: report the trend, never praise or warn. The weight feature is opt-in and its copy says how to hide it and delete it.

## Honesty lines

- Every average names its days: "Averages use 18 of 30 days (12 partial or not logged days left out)."
- Provenance words are fixed: "Your numbers", "Estimated". Confidence words are fixed: "Low", "Medium", "High" (plus "confidence" outside rows).
- Explain an estimate in one line: "Claude estimated all values from a photo. Portion size and cooking oil were not stated."
- The integration status is always a fact with a time: "Last meal saved 2 h ago via Claude", "No meals saved in the last 2 days".

## Formats

- Times: the person's system setting (24 h "12:40", or "12:40 PM"). Relative: "just now", "20 min ago", "2 h ago", "yesterday at 20:15".
- Dates: "Sunday, October 18" in the Today header, "Oct 12–18" for weeks, "October 2026" for months.
- Units: kcal or kJ, g, mg, µg, kg or lb, always after a space. Ranges use an en dash without spaces.
- Never use the em dash in UI copy; use a period, a comma or parentheses.

## Privacy copy

- Say where data lives: "on food.example.com", "on your server".
- Destructive confirmations list exactly what goes: "214 meals, 22 weight entries, targets and settings, 2 connected assistants. It can't be undone."
- Remind people that their other logs are untouched: "Data you logged elsewhere, for example in a ChatGPT project or a spreadsheet, is not touched."
